# Reproducibility scope

This repository publishes redistributable derived artifacts used to support the SAREF manuscript.

The public package currently includes aggregated condition-level outcomes, fixed-strategy summaries, propagation summaries, action-set ablation results, leave-one-failure-class-out sensitivity results, and the failure/recovery taxonomy.

The upstream InterCode benchmark is not redistributed here. Users should obtain InterCode from the official project/repository and comply with its licensing and environment setup requirements.

The manuscript reports 13 retained InterCode Bash tasks, 39 controlled failure episodes, and 117 matched recovery branches. The public files in this repository are derived summaries of that frozen experiment. They should not be interpreted as a population sample of deployed AI-agent failures.

Additional redistributable branch-level artifacts may be added after a final redistribution and privacy review.
